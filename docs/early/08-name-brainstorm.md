# Name brainstorm

A name has to be:

- Short (≤ 9 letters ideally).
- Pronounceable in both English and Russian.
- Available as `.dev` (and ideally `.ru`).
- Not trademark-trapped in the smart-home / sensing space.
- Brandable — works as a logo, works as a verb ("I just WaveSight'd the room").

## Candidates (raw dump)

- **WaveSight** — "see waves". Short, brandable, very obvious meaning.
- **AetherSense** — mystical, RF-coded. Longer.
- **EchoMesh** — emphasises mesh, but conflict with audio "echo".
- **Halo** — single word, overloaded with brands (Halo Health, Halo game).
- **Prisma** — pretty but generic.
- **Lumen** — light pun for RF; nice, but Lumen also a power-meter brand.
- **Resonance** — too long, vague.
- **Pulse** — overloaded.
- **Radian** — geometric, but "Radiant" is everywhere.
- **Anima** — too literary.
- **Spectra** — generic, hard to own.
- **RFlux** — too engineery, not brandable.
- **Whisper** — too quiet, also Cloudflare uses similar.
- **Phantom** — connotation problems.
- **Sage** — taken many times.
- **Tessera** — beautiful but obscure.
- **Bloom** — too generic, gardening apps own it.

## Filter pass 1 — too generic / overloaded

- Halo, Pulse, Sage, Spectra, Lumen, Bloom, Phantom — out.

## Filter pass 2 — domain available + clean meaning

- **WaveSight** — `wavesight.dev` checked, free. Russian-pronounceable
  ("вэйвсайт" / "вэйв-сайт").
- **AetherSense** — `aethersense.io` free, `.dev` not. Pronunciation in
  RU is awkward.
- **Prisma** — likely taken.
- **Anima** — taken in healthcare.

Will sleep on this and pick on Monday.
