# Rough budget

## EN

A hobby project should not bankrupt me. Setting an envelope.

### Hardware (one-off)

- 4 × ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1: $40
- USB cables + hub: $24
- Used RGB-D camera (for eval ground truth): $60
- 2 × DWM3000 UWB (later, for M6): $50
- Raspberry Pi 5 8 GB (later upgrade from Pi 4): $80
- Miscellaneous (wires, mounts, 3D-printed cases): $30

**Hardware subtotal: ~$284**

### Recurring

- HuggingFace storage for datasets: free tier should cover M1-M5.
- Domain `wavesight.dev` + `wavesight.ru`: ~$30/year combined.
- GitHub: free.
- Discord server: free.

**Recurring: ~$30/year**

### Capped budget

I am giving myself **$500 over the first year**. If I hit it, I stop
and review whether it's worth continuing.

### Time budget

Evenings and weekends. Approximately 10 hours / week. If I average 8
real hours of focused work weekly, that is ~400 hours over 12 months.
That should be enough to get to M5 (sleep + smart-home) at honest
quality.
